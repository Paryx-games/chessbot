# Code of conduct

I welcome anyone who wants to contribute, whatever their background or experience level. Please treat people with respect, give constructive feedback, and discuss technical disagreements using evidence.

I don't allow harassment, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, sharing someone's private information, or sustained disruption in this project's spaces.

I may remove harmful content or restrict participation. If you have a concern, contact me through a private contact method I've listed on GitHub. If you can't find one, ask me for a private channel without posting personal details. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

I've based this on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
